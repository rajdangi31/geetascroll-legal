Scroll Gita Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Scroll Gita (package app.geetascroll.mobile) is an offline-first reading and audio app for the Bhagavad Gita. This policy describes the data the app and its API handle.

What we collect
- A random installation identifier minted on first launch so likes, saves, and reading progress can sync.
- Feed, sync, and reliability events generated while using the app, including card impressions and crash diagnostics.
- Purchase entitlement status when you choose Scroll Gita Plus or offer a voluntary Dakshina via Google Play Billing. Payment details are processed exclusively by Google Play.

What we do not collect
- Your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.
- Cross-app advertising tracking profiles.

How we use data
- To render your feed, remember likes, saves, language preferences, reminders, and reading streaks in local SQLite on your device.
- To operate the API, detect abuse, and improve reliability.
- Crash and performance events help us find jank and startup errors.

Where data lives
- The full Bhagavad Gita catalog, along with your likes, saves, streaks, and reading progress, is stored locally on your device first.
- Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device using Android notifications.
- When our service is reachable, sync mutations are sent over HTTPS to our backend database.

Sharing
- We do not sell personal data.
- Infrastructure hosting providers and Google Play Billing process data strictly to operate the app, subscriptions, and content delivery.

Retention & deletion
- Installation credentials expire and can be rotated.
- Open Settings → Reset local data & installation in the app (or clear app storage in Android settings) to erase the local database, cache, and installation credential immediately.
- To request deletion of server-synced installation records, email privacy@geetascroll.app with your diagnostic ID from Settings.

Children
- Scroll Gita is not directed at children under 13.
