GeetaScroll Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

GeetaScroll is an offline-first reading app for the Bhagavad Gita. This policy describes the data the app and its API handle.

What we collect
- A random installation identifier minted on first launch so likes, saves, and reading progress can sync.
- Feed, sync, and reliability events generated while using the app, including card impressions and crash diagnostics.
- Optional account identifiers only if you later link this installation to an account. This release does not require an account.

What we do not collect
- Your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.
- Advertising identifiers for ads. GeetaScroll does not show third-party ads.

How we use data
- To render your feed, remember likes and saves, and keep a local SQLite copy on the device.
- To operate the API, detect abuse, and improve reliability.
- Crash and performance events help us find jank and startup errors.

Where data lives
- The full Bhagavad Gita catalog, along with your likes, saves, and reading progress, is stored locally on your device first.
- When the GeetaScroll service is reachable, sync mutations are sent over HTTPS to our backend database.

Sharing
- We do not sell personal data.
- Infrastructure hosting providers process data strictly to operate the API, database, and content delivery network.

Retention & deletion
- Installation credentials expire and can be rotated.
- Open Settings → Reset local data & installation in the app (or clear app storage in Android settings) to erase the local database, cache, and installation credential immediately.
- To request deletion of server-synced installation records, email privacy@geetascroll.app with your diagnostic ID from Settings.

Children
- GeetaScroll is not directed at children under 13.
